### Study Rationale & Clinical Importance to General Internal Medicine
Cardiovascular-Kidney-Metabolic (CKM) syndrome, codified by the American Heart Association in 2023, represents a major public health challenge affecting millions of patients managed across primary care and general internal medicine practices. While metabolic dysfunction-associated steatotic liver disease (MASLD) is acknowledged as a key metabolic driver, current clinical practice guidelines (e.g., AASLD and ADA) recommend the formula-based Fibrosis-4 (FIB-4) index as the universal first-line screening tool for hepatic fibrosis in ambulatory primary care.

However, FIB-4 has a fundamental architectural vulnerability: chronological age is explicitly positioned in the formula numerator. Using the continuous, newly harmonized CDC National Health and Nutrition Examination Survey (NHANES) 2017–March 2020 and 2021–2023 cycles, we conducted a nationally representative investigation to evaluate direct physical liver stiffness measured via vibration-controlled transient elastography (VCTE; FibroScan) and high-sensitivity C-reactive protein (hs-CRP), alongside a rigorous head-to-head diagnostic comparison against FIB-4:

1. **Statistically Significant Synergism:** Physical liver stiffness ($\ge 8.0$ kPa) and systemic vascular inflammation (hs-CRP $\ge 2.0$ mg/L) act in pronounced supra-additive and supra-multiplicative synergy to double the odds of advanced CKM syndrome (Model 2 aOR: 2.01, 95% CI: 1.39–2.92; multiplicative $p = 0.0185$; odds-scale RERI: 0.91, 95% CI: 0.19–1.63; odds-scale AP: 0.45; standardized prevalence interaction: $+5.9$ percentage points, 95% CI: 1.3–10.5; prevalence-scale RERI: 0.47, AP: 31.0%). These interaction metrics reflect non-causal statistical effect modification on the odds and absolute probability scales.
2. **Liver Fibrosis as an Inflammatory Multiplier:** Isolated physical liver stiffness without systemic inflammation did not significantly increase advanced CKM risk (aOR: 0.85, 95% CI: 0.53–1.35), a pattern consistent with statistical interaction but insufficient to establish biological causation.
3. **Severe Geriatric Misclassification by FIB-4:** In a direct head-to-head comparison against physical elastography, FIB-4 exhibited an overall sensitivity of only 34.8% and a positive predictive value of 14.5%. Most critically for general internists, **in adults aged $\ge 65$ years, the false-positive rate of FIB-4 escalated 4.5-fold to 63.8%** (compared to 14.3% in younger adults). These false-positive rates use VCTE stiffness as the reference rather than biopsy-confirmed fibrosis. Age calibration reduces false positives while lowering sensitivity; the study does not establish age-independent VCTE accuracy.
